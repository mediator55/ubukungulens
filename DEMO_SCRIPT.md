# 2 minute 30 second demo script

**0:00–0:20 — Introduce the problem**  
“UbukunguLens helps people explore published evidence about financial inclusion in Rwanda. Overall inclusion is high, but access, account ownership, and usage are different questions.”

**0:20–0:55 — Executive overview**  
Show the four headline indicators and gender comparison. Say: “The published transactional-account figures show 73% for female adults and 82% for male adults, a descriptive difference of 9 percentage points. These figures do not explain the cause.”

**0:55–1:25 — Explore indicators**  
Choose a theme, change the indicators, hover over the chart, inspect the definition table, open a source link, and show CSV export.

**1:25–1:50 — Action planner**  
Change the hypothetical group size and target. Explain: “This is arithmetic for planning only, not an estimate of policy impact.”

**1:50–2:20 — Data and methodology**  
Show official source links and limitations. Explain that the current prototype uses published aggregate data and keeps EICV7 poverty separate rather than claiming an unsupported link to FinScope groups.

**2:20–2:30 — Close**  
“The next step is authorized microdata analysis with documented survey weights, definitions, and uncertainty. UbukunguLens prioritizes transparency and reproducibility.”
